What It Is & How It Works in Real Life

🛡️ 1. Driver Privacy Shield

In standard lots, raw license plates are stored where rogue employees or hackers could track your daily routines. Our edge box blurs and hashes plates immediately upon entry.

🔒 2. Tamper-Proof Sensors

Outdoor ultrasonic sensors sign every reading with ECC Digital Signatures. If an attacker tries to inject fake "empty" parking spots to cause traffic jams, the edge box discards the packet.

🍯 3. The Thief Decoy Trap

When an intruder attempts to brute-force a payment terminal, normal kiosks say "Access Denied". Our edge box uses Honey Encryption to feed the attacker realistic fake credit cards, catching them without alerting them!

📊 4. Smart Math with No Peeking

The city grid needs to calculate total EV charging load and total free spots across city boxes. Using Homomorphic Encryption, the boxes add numbers together while remaining completely encrypted!
